All individual observations and plans are fictional demonstrations. Imported declarations are not authenticated. Matching protocol/acquisition strings cannot establish instrument accuracy, uncertainty or physiological comparability. Software support describes a narrow recorded comparison; it does not imply safety, causality, clinical significance, overall function, EVA clearance or readiness.

Only a small measurement catalogue is implemented. Manual entry and JSON import do not constitute device integration. Two HR readings cannot establish aerobic capacity. No personalised treatment, triage, medication, exercise intensity or rehabilitation guidance is generated. Existing mission procedures and qualified personnel govern real care. Earth posture/locomotor assessments are not recommended as routine microgravity tests.

Published findings come from small, unequal cohorts and pooled session changes. Group associations cannot supply individual outcomes. Nonsignificance does not prove equivalence. No participant trajectories, exact unavailable confidence intervals or inaccessible supplement values are reconstructed. Bed rest is an analog with limits on transfer to flight.

Offline work requires a prior successful installation in the same browser and address. External sources need connectivity. Local storage can be cleared or unavailable. Markdown, HTML and JSON handoffs cannot restore a session. The application is not a secure medical-record system, communications channel, immutable clinical audit trail or spacecraft-qualified installation.

The tests use synthetic cases. Shared authorship of implementation, rubric and source transcription limits independence. Automated accessibility checks do not replace assistive-technology testing or human evaluation. Human pilot N = 0; scored N = 0. No clinical, flight, astronaut-usability or comparative-benefit claim is supported.

The mission foundation and the retained guided candidate have not received independent team acceptance. Public release also requires review of historical development timing/reuse, applicable competition rules and distribution rights. Local source preparation does not resolve those decisions. Third-party scientific and photographic material retains its own rights, including identifiable-person and endorsement constraints.

An original Level 1 commercial Astroskin-format adapter processes synthetic HR/quality JSON; a bounded foreground replay and separate same-profile companion exchange persisted event receipts and acknowledgments. No physical device or live monitoring stream is connected. The editable disposition is an explicitly fictional workflow action. Its three review questions are not an exhaustive medical checklist. There is no validated Mars balance/mobility acquisition protocol here; no physical self-test is offered. A change in review phase never changes acquisition gravity. The historical locked evaluation protocol does not assess this new companion experience.

Only the existing fictional cycling archive record can be collected through the mission planner. The other catalogue options require equipment, an approved method, qualified assistance or a later environment that the scenario does not establish. No new physical assessment, browser medical test, VO2max estimate or substitute for functional evidence is provided. Source matching verifies a bundled fictional document, not instrument accuracy or authenticity of a real clinical record. Schema 1 mission saves explicitly reset; unrelated guided/workspace data remain separate. Unique session IDs prevent restart revision collisions, while simultaneous tabs can still overwrite each other's local state.

Health interpretation uses fixed source-linked templates. The higher later exercise HR has no established cause or clinical significance. Source-format validity, reported signal quality and clinical interpretation remain separate; see DEVICE_FORMATS.md.

Fictional reviews record bounded disposition/reason/priority selections under a fixed demo role. They authenticate no physician and grant no mission clearance. Saved snapshots are immutable within the application model, with stale currency after evidence/context or relevant version changes; local browser storage remains editable, evictable and unencrypted.
