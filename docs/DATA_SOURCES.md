# Data sources and reuse boundaries

The public application uses independently authored fictional examples and attributed study-level numerical facts. It contains no participant-level NASA health records or derived participant predictions. A label saying “synthetic” cannot de-identify real data supplied by a user; real records must not be entered.

Scientific paths in this table are relative to `docs/`. These small generated/curated scientific registers are versioned because tests and reviewers need the exact source transcriptions, units and provenance. They are reproducibility inputs; production bundles use the separately typed TypeScript data. Build output, screenshots, reports and ZIP files are generated and excluded from Git.

Primary quantitative reference: Mulavara et al. (2018), _Physiological and Functional Alterations after Spaceflight and Bed Rest_, DOI [10.1249/MSS.0000000000001615](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000001615), [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6133205/), [Europe PMC XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC6133205/fullTextXML). Study design context also uses Cromwell et al. (2018), [Overview of the NASA 70-day Bed Rest Study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6591716/). See the catalogue for every NASA source used by the interface.

The numerical facts are attributed and drawn as original accessible charts. No blanket redistribution permission is asserted for publications, supplements, NASA-hosted third-party assets or human data. Historical participant research, exploratory analyses and unresolved-rights captures are excluded from the application repository and all public source packages. Their access and redistribution questions remain unresolved; they are unnecessary for ordinary reproduction.

The NTRS concept report [20220017856](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20220017856) is used only at catalogue/abstract scope. Its publication year is 2021; 2022 is the catalogue acquisition year. The retained link-check record distinguishes blocked direct/API requests from readable catalogue metadata. Supplement retrieval failures and unavailable confidence-interval values remain documented; no missing values were reconstructed.

Mission source locators are also embedded in the three typed review requirements in `web/src/mission/requirements.ts`. They link to NASA Astronaut Exercise (equipment context), NASA Human Performance V1 4002/4003 (programme requirements), and Clément et al. (2022) Methods/Discussion (Earth-return research). Only short attributed factual summaries are distributed, without article images or copied documents. The mission now links to a separate published-group aggregate demonstration and an original Level 1 commercial Astroskin-format synthetic HR/quality adapter. Neither supplies individual assessment evidence. No Bluetooth, physical hardware or vendor service is connected.

## Commercial formats and licensed aggregate input

Commercial Astroskin documentation (API reference labelled 3.3.x), reviewed 14 November 2026, defines the implemented HR 19/quality 1000 JSON subset. [DEVICE_FORMATS.md](DEVICE_FORMATS.md) records sources, mapping, clock ambiguity, original implementation basis, synthetic origin and Level 1 limits. Public documentation is used for technical facts; no vendor SDK, recording, service response or documentation capture is distributed.

Clément G, Moudy SC, Macaulay TR, Mulder E and Wood SJ (2024), _Effects of intermittent seating upright, lower body negative pressure, and exercise on functional tasks performance after head-down tilt bed rest_, Frontiers in Physiology 15:1442239, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2024.1442239), [Table 3](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2024.1442239/full#T3), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The authors and copyright holders receive attribution in the UI and numerical register. No table-specific third-party exclusion was identified.

[CLEMENT2024_TANDEM.json](science/CLEMENT2024_TANDEM.json) transcribes the eight tandem endpoint/group rows (means, SDs and reported p cells), population, group labels and method. The original function in web/src/research/aggregate.ts calculates differences of rounded means in percentage points. It produces no new significance tests or confidence intervals. The Table 4 unit-label discrepancy is disclosed; Table 3 and Methods define this endpoint. Ground-based 30-day bed-rest group summaries cannot predict individual Mars outcomes.

## Fictional register v2

The versioned `fictional-health-register-v2` is authored synthetic data, not NASA participant data, device exports or collected visitor information. Seven additional dated records have typed provenance and full acquisition declarations in `web/src/mission/richEvidence.ts`; definitions and comparison limits are in Methods. A separate versioned descriptive surface crew report appears only in the Surface chapter and never satisfies a mobility measurement. Research source additions are registered in Science and in canonical review source entries.
