# Use of Super Intelligence (SI) in PARALLAX

PARALLAX was built with substantial help from SI tools: **ChatGPT (OpenAI), OpenAI Codex and Claude Code (Anthropic)**.

| Area | What SI did | What the team did |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Found, summarised and cross-checked NASA, CSA and published sources | Chose the sources the app relies on and reviewed the cited primary pages |
| Idea development | Helped refine the concept, narrative and scientific boundaries; critiqued drafts | The available records do not establish original idea authorship or pre-event timing; the team must confirm these facts before making competition representations. |
| Design | Proposed layouts, wording, the guided-demo flow and accessibility fixes | Set the direction and accepted or rejected changes |
| Code | Generated and edited most of the TypeScript/React code, tests and build tooling in phased development cycles | Wrote the phase instructions, set acceptance criteria and reviewed results |
| Testing | Wrote and ran unit, scenario and Chromium tests; produced verification reports | Decided acceptance |
| Data | Authored the fictional mission records and synthetic wearable frames, all labelled fictional or synthetic | Defined the scenario |
| Images | Original AI-generated habitat and equipment illustrations added for the public edition; original procedural Mars SVG; project diagrams. Illustrations are labelled in captions and alt text | — |
| Writing | Drafted and edited documentation, the project page and the presentation | Reviewed and approved the final text |

No audio or video was generated. No SI-generated content uses NASA branding. The application uses no AI model at runtime and sends no data to any AI service. Product screenshots are real captures of the application.

The sole human contributor is Brendan Lim, as confirmed on 16 November 2026. The public edition contains no agency photographs. Original illustration prompts and provenance are in docs/ILLUSTRATION_PROMPTS.md and docs/IMAGE_ATTRIBUTIONS.json.
